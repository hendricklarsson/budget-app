Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Budget Tracker is a free personal portfolio project, not a commercial service. This page explains what it stores, where, and how you can delete it.

What the app stores

  • Your Google email address, from "Sign in with Google", to keep your entries separate from everyone else's. The app does not ask Google for anything else (no contacts, files or calendar).
  • The expenses you enter: amount, category, date and note.
  • Receipt photos, if you add them. Photos are shrunk on your device before upload.

Where it is stored

Your entries are stored in a Google Sheet, and your receipt photos in a private Google Drive folder, both in the developer's personal Google account. They are not shared with anyone.

On your own device, the app keeps a copy of your entries and your sign-in so it opens quickly. Signing out removes them from the device.

Who can see it

In the app, only you can see your data: every request is checked against your Google sign-in, and you only ever get your own entries back. As the owner of the Sheet and Drive folder, the developer can technically see the stored data, and only looks at it when needed to fix a problem.

Your data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Deleting an entry in History removes it from the Sheet and moves its receipt photo to the trash. To remove everything at once, open Settings → Delete my data at any time. Photos in the trash are deleted permanently by Google within 30 days.

Fair-use limits

To keep this free service running, each person can add up to 200 entries and 50 receipt photos a day. The app counts how many you've added today, and nothing else about how you use it.

Google Sign-In

Signing in is handled by Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy.
