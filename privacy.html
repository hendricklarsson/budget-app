Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Sen Tracker is a free personal portfolio project, not a commercial service. This page explains what it stores, where, who can read it, and how you can delete it.

You can also try the app with Try the Demo: the demo uses sample data only, sends nothing to the server and keeps nothing after you leave.

Encrypted on Your Phone

Before anything leaves your phone, Sen Tracker encrypts each entry's amount, category, date, time and note, and your receipt photos, with a key that only your passphrase or your recovery code can unlock. Your passphrase and recovery code never leave your phone. The server stores only the scrambled result, so the developer can't read your entries or see your photos.

This also means nobody can reset your passphrase for you. If you forget both your passphrase and your recovery code, your entries can't be opened by anyone, and the only way forward is to delete them and start fresh.

What the Developer Can See

  • Your Google email address, from "Sign in with Google", to keep your entries separate from everyone else's. The app does not ask Google for anything else (no contacts, files or calendar).
  • How many entries you have and when each was created.
  • The size of each receipt photo (not the photo itself).
  • Your locked key box: your key, locked with your passphrase and recovery code. Without one of those it can't be opened.

Where It Is Stored

The encrypted entries are stored in a Google Sheet, and the encrypted receipt photos in a private Google Drive folder, both in the developer's personal Google account. They are not shared with anyone.

On your own device, the app keeps an encrypted copy of your entries so it opens quickly, even offline. With Remember on This Device on, it also keeps your key on this device, so you don't have to type your passphrase each time. Settings → Forget This Device removes it. The name you choose in Settings is kept on your device only.

Entries From Before Encryption

Entries added before encryption arrived (November 2026) were stored readable. When you make your passphrase, the app encrypts them and replaces the readable copies, and their photos are moved to the trash, which Google empties within 30 days.

Never Sold or Shared

Your data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting an entry in History removes it from the Sheet and moves its photo to the trash. To remove everything at once, including your locked key box, open Settings → Delete Account at any time. Photos in the trash are deleted permanently by Google within 30 days.

Fair-Use Limits

To keep this free service running, each person can add up to 200 entries and 50 receipt photos a day. The app counts how many you've added today, and nothing else about how you use it.

Google Sign-In

Signing in is handled by Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy.
